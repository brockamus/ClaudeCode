Good to know

Questions and terms

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your photos are yours.

Do I need a domain name?

If you already have one, we connect it. If not, we help you register one in your name.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down between plans whenever your needs change.

When do monthly payments start?

On launch day. You never pay for a plan while your site is still being built.

What counts as a small update?

Things like new hours, a price change, fresh photos or a new testimonial. Bigger additions, like new pages, are quoted separately on the Hosting and Automations plans.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are billed monthly.
