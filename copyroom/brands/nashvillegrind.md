# Nashville Grind — brand voice guide

## Identity (canonical line)
Nashville Grind is a Nashville coffee roaster — "Roasted Fresh in Nashville" — selling bags, pods, merch, and subscriptions at nashvillegrind.com. Music City personality baked into every blend name and tasting note. (Konversly agency client — Brock does not own this brand.)

## Voice
- Southern, musical, hard-working: blend names and tasting notes mix real flavor language with Nashville/country flavor ("Rockin' Country Song," "Sweet Smell of Victory," "Elbow Grease").
- Warm and playful, not precious third-wave coffee-snob copy. "Live to Grind" is the lifestyle line (About page imagery: musicians, farmers, porch life).
- Community angle: the Almost Famous Fund is a brand-truth trust element.

## Audience
Nashville locals + country/Americana-identity coffee drinkers nationwide; subscription-minded daily drinkers; TikTok Shop browsers.

## Offers (strict rules)
- SUBSCRIBE & SAVE 10% — genuine 10% selling plan, 3 delivery frequencies (Seal Subscriptions). Subscribe card = "BEST VALUE." This is the only verified offer; don't invent others.

## Key angles that work
- Roasted fresh in Nashville (locality + freshness).
- Subscription as the default smart choice (10% + never run out).
- Blend-personality storytelling — each blend has a character; lean on the tasting-note trios.
- Almost Famous Fund community support.

## Banned / forbidden
- NO founding year / "ESTD." dates — no real founding year is on file; AI images fabricating years (2004/2024) was a known defect, not fact.
- NO review counts/star ratings in copy — no confirmed review source exists (ReviewXpo widget shows 0 reviews). Never fabricate social proof.
- Don't misstate bag size: 12 OZ / 340 G.
- Don't drift into generic "artisan single-origin" specialty-coffee voice — this brand is personality-forward Nashville, not pour-over pretension.
