### Post Title: Why Our Family Chose Natural Health Over Pharmaceuticals

Three years into homesteading and I've realized something: once you start questioning where your food comes from, you inevitably start questioning where your medicine comes from too.

We grow our own vegetables. We raise our own chickens. We generate our own power. So when my doctor handed me a prescription for a $1,200/month injection to manage post-baby weight, I asked the same question I ask about everything: *is there a way to do this ourselves?*

Turns out there is.

I've spent the last year researching natural alternatives to GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic. The science is actually fascinating — your body already produces the hormones these drugs mimic. The question is whether you can support that natural production instead of replacing it with a synthetic version.

I've been documenting everything I've learned on my main blog:

→ [The Complete Guide to Natural GLP-3](https://homesteadfanatic.com/glp3-the-complete-guide-to-natural-weight-management-for-homesteaders/)
→ [Natural Health on the Homestead](https://homesteadfanatic.com/natural-health-homestead-wellness-guide/)
→ [Our Full Homesteading Journey](https://homesteadfanatic.com/about/)

The short version: natural GLP-3 supplements support your body's own hormone production. No injection, no prescription, no $1,200 monthly bill. Is it as dramatic as the pharmaceutical version? No. But it fits the way we live — working with our bodies, not replacing what they can do.

**Subject:** The 3 things I'd do first if I were starting over

If I lost everything tomorrow and had to rebuild our homestead from scratch, here's exactly what I'd prioritize — in order.

**1. Food security (month 1)**

Not a massive garden. Not 50 chickens. Just the basics: a 3-month emergency food supply built gradually from grocery store runs, plus a small raised bed garden with high-calorie, easy-to-grow crops (potatoes, beans, squash, tomatoes).

Full guide: [How to Build a 6-Month Emergency Food Supply on a Budget](https://homesteadfanatic.com/emergency-food-supply-budget/)

**2. Water independence (month 2)**

A rain barrel system and a quality water filter. You can survive weeks without food but only days without water. And when the power goes out, your well pump doesn't work.

Guide: [Water Purification Methods for Any Emergency](https://homesteadfanatic.com/water-purification-methods/)

**3. Health self-reliance (month 3)**

A natural medicine cabinet, a solid first aid kit, and the knowledge to use both. This is the piece most preppers skip, and it's the one that will matter most in a real emergency.

Guides:
- [Natural Health on the Homestead](https://homesteadfanatic.com/natural-health-homestead-wellness-guide/)
- [How to Build a Survival Medical Kit](https://homesteadfanatic.com/how-to-build-a-survival-medical-kit-complete-checklist-for-emergencies/)
- [Best Natural Medicine Books](https://homesteadfanatic.com/best-natural-medicine-books/)

Everything else — chickens, solar, off-grid systems — comes after these three foundations are in place.
