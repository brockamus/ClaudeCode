## Ground rules

1. **Post from your real personal account** — not a company account. Personal accounts get more reach + trust.
2. **Disclose founder status** where platform conventions require (Reddit, Quora, HubPages).
3. **One link per piece** — adding a second link signals self-promo, triggers moderation.
4. **Don't publish Medium + Substack on the same day** — Google's link graph will see it as coordinated.
5. **Don't use alt accounts** to boost engagement. Ever. Bannable offense on every platform.
6. **Respond to comments** for 48 hours after posting. Engagement signals real authorship.
7. **Track everything** in a spreadsheet (see Tier 4 checklist for template).

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Pay influencers for followed links (Google penalizes; FTC requires disclosure)
