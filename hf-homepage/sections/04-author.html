Meet Sarah Kate.

I'm a mom of three and the voice behind Homestead Fanatic. Five years ago I was a suburban mom in Texas with a Costco membership and zero homesteading skills. Then the 2021 ice storm hit — four days without power, heat, or running water with a newborn. That night changed everything. Today my family lives on 12 acres outside Wimberley, and this site is the resource I wish I'd had when I started.
