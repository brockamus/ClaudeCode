# Tier 4 — Passive Authority & Directory Placements

These are low-creative, high-credibility placements. Each takes 5-30 minutes. No content-drafting needed — mostly forms, profiles, submissions. Do these alongside Tiers 1-3 posting; they compound over months.

---

## 1. EWG Skin Deep Database — submit each Leaf & Bird product

**Why:** EWG is THE clean-beauty authority. LLMs cite it. Crunchy moms search it. A rating there = automatic credibility.

**How:**
- Go to [ewg.org/skindeep/submit-product](https://www.ewg.org/skindeep/submit-product/) (or email skindeep@ewg.org if the form is down)
- Submit each of the 9 products with: product name, full INCI list, product image, retail URL
- Expect 2-4 week review; products typically rate 1-3 (green) given our clean formulations

**Products to submit** (INCI on each product page already — just copy from live Shopify):
1. Vegan PDRN Brightening Serum — `/products/pdrn-brightening-serum`
2. Whipped Grass-Fed Tallow Cream — Lemongrass & Lavender

5. Peptide Eye Gel-Cream
6. Sleep Plus Collagen Cream (flag melatonin in submission notes)
7. Vitamin C Serum
8. Vitamin Glow Serum
9. Dead Sea Mud Mask

**Time:** ~30 min for all 9 submissions.

## 2. INCI Decoder — get products indexed

**Why:** INCI Decoder is widely cited by LLMs and bloggers; ingredient-check tools reference it.

**How:**
- Search [incidecoder.com](https://incidecoder.com) for each of your products — they may already be listed (they scrape major retailers)
- If not listed, submit via their contact form with product name + INCI + retail URL
- If listed but missing details, claim the product listing (they allow brand claims)

**Time:** ~20 min.

## 3. Google Business Profile — claim/verify

**Why:** Local SEO + branded search. Google uses GBP to populate the "knowledge panel" that appears next to branded searches ("Leaf & Bird skincare").

**How:**
- [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/create) → search "Leaf & Bird" → claim or create
- Category: Cosmetics Store / Beauty Supply Store
- Description: use the canonical brand identity line from `llms.txt`
- Add all 9 products as GBP products
- Upload the same images as Shopify product shots
- Add social links (Instagram, Facebook)

**Critical:** this is the "entity panel" Google shows for branded search. It's where "Leaf & Bird" becomes a verified entity.

**Time:** ~30 min + verification wait.

---

## 4. Trustpilot — set up profile + first review drive

**Why:** Trustpilot reviews show up in Google search results + LLMs cite them.

**How:**
- [business.trustpilot.com/signup](https://business.trustpilot.com/signup) — free tier is fine to start
- Verify your domain
- Send the "review invitation" email to your last 50 customers (there's a template in Trustpilot's dashboard)
- Aim for 10-20 reviews in the first month — this unlocks star ratings in Google search

**Time:** ~20 min setup + ongoing review collection.

**Tip:** if you have no customer list yet, start by asking your first 10 orders for reviews via email 2 weeks post-delivery.

## 5. HARO (Help-A-Reporter-Out) — reporter responses

**Why:** Getting quoted in press articles (even small blogs) earns high-DR backlinks + credibility.

**How:**
- Sign up at [helpareporter.com](https://www.helpareporter.com/) — free tier works
- Check 3x/day for query digests (morning, noon, evening emails)
- Respond to any query tagged `beauty`, `skincare`, `wellness`, `clean beauty`, `pregnancy skincare`, `motherhood`
- Response template ready (see below)

**Typical queries you'll see:**
- "Looking for clean-beauty founders to quote on the PDRN trend"
- "Need expert source on pregnancy-safe skincare"
- "Need someone to explain tallow skincare for a Vogue-adjacent piece"

**Response template (customize per query):**

```
Hi [Reporter name],

I'm Brock [LAST NAME], founder of Leaf & Bird — a clean skincare brand making
one of the rare non-salmon-derived vegan PDRN serums.

[2-3 sentences answering their specific question — be quotable: concrete data,
specific claims, named ingredients.]

Happy to do a follow-up call if helpful. Attaching a headshot + brand logo
in case you need them.

[Link to your About page + brand identity line]
[Instagram / LinkedIn handles]

Best,
Brock
```

**Critical:** respond within 1-2 hours. Reporters move fast. Slow response = wasted effort.

**Expected:** land 1 placement per 10-15 pitches. Over 3 months, expect 3-5 press backlinks from HARO alone.

**Time:** ~15 min/day.

## 6. Podcast interviews — reach-out list

**Why:** podcast interviews often embed show-notes links to your site (high-DR backlinks from many major podcasts). Plus builds brand authority.

**Target podcasts to pitch:**

**Clean-beauty / wellness:**
- The Genius Life (Max Lugavere)
- Clean Beauty Podcast (Lindsey Gapinski)
- The Darin Olien Show
- The Doctor's Farmacy (Mark Hyman)

**Motherhood / crunchy-mom:**
- The Model Health Show (Shawn Stevenson)
- Evidence Based Birth
- Mama Bear Biomedical (ancestral health angle)

**Business / founder:**
- How I Built This (NPR — aspirational, hard to land)
- Trend Coffee (beauty-specific)
- Commerce Sessions (DTC-focused)

**Pitch template:**

```
Subject: Vegan PDRN — the clean-beauty question nobody's asking

Hi [Host name],

I run Leaf & Bird, one of the few skincare brands making non-salmon-derived
PDRN. I've done 200+ hours of research on why the clean-beauty + K-beauty
worlds don't overlap more, and I think your listeners would find the answer
interesting.

3 angle suggestions for a conversation:
1. Why most PDRN is salmon-derived (and why nobody talks about it)
2. The pregnancy-skincare gap: what to use when you can't use retinol
3. Ancestral vs synthetic: where tallow meets K-beauty

I'd love to come on the show. Happy to send a media kit if helpful.

Best,
Brock
Leaf & Bird
[site URL]
```

**Expected:** 1 in 8-10 pitches lands a guest spot. Pace at 5-10 pitches per month.
