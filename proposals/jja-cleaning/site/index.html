Good questions

Frequently asked

Are quotes really free?

Yes. Call or text 316-350-7493, or send the quote form, and we’ll give you a free estimate before any work starts.

Are you actually available 24/7?

Yes. No matter the time or day, we’ll be there when you need us, including early-morning Airbnb turnovers and after-hours office cleans.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Augusta, KS and serve homes and businesses across Butler and Sedgwick counties, including Augusta, Wichita, El Dorado, Andover, Derby, Towanda, Rose Hill, Benton, Bel Aire, Park City, Haysville, Mulvane, Maize, Goddard, Valley Center and Douglass. Nearby but not listed? Text us your address.

Can I set up recurring cleaning?

Yes. Choose daily, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly service, or book a one-time clean.

Do you handle Airbnb and rental turnovers?

Yes. We offer fast, thorough turnover cleaning for short-term rentals, plus ongoing cleaning for property managers and realtors.

What if what I need isn’t listed?

Ask. If it has to do with cleaning, we’re there for whatever you may need, and we build custom plans for unique jobs.
