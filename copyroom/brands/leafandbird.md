# Leaf & Bird — brand voice guide

## Identity (canonical line)
Leaf & Bird is clean and ancestral beauty — rare non-salmon-derived vegan PDRN serum and whipped grass-fed tallow creams for the ingredient-literate, clean-beauty skeptics who read every label.

Never call the BRAND "vegan" (it sells grass-fed tallow). Vegan describes specific products only.

## Voice
- Hybrid voice by product line: **PDRN/actives → clinical, K-beauty-grade, clean-active framing.** **Tallow → warm, ancestral, "what our grandmothers used," grass-fed/pasture-raised.** Crunchy/ingredient-literate persona unifies both.
- Honest disclosures over marketing polish — this audience rewards transparency and punishes greenwashing. Address concerns head-on (e.g., PDRN serum contains phenoxyethanol; bergamot cream is photosensitizing — wear SPF; Sleep Plus contains melatonin and lavender oil, flagged for pregnancy).
- Speak to identity, not demographic: "the ingredient-literate," people who read every INCI and cross-reference EWG. Do NOT say "for moms" / "health-conscious moms" in general copy. Pregnancy-safe is a product USP, not a demographic gate. (Exception: ad copy bought against a pregnancy/postpartum audience may use first-person mom-to-mom voice.)
- No hype-y AI-sounding cadence. Concrete claims, plain language, label-reader energy.

## Audience
Predominantly women 28–55, often pregnancy / postpartum / perimenopause life stages (targeting only — keep site copy psychographic). Burned by greenwashing; skeptical of "clean" claims; Reddit-skincare literate.

## Offers (strict rules)
- **CLEAN15 = 15% off FIRST ORDER ONLY.** The only discount ever mentioned. Never imply a sitewide sale or that prices are marked down.
- Free shipping $50+. "The Clean-Skin Promise": 30-day refund.
- Bundles are the value play (25–35% off vs singles) — push bundles instead of discounts.

## Key angles that work
- "Pregnancy-safe retinol alternative" (PDRN vs retinol)
- "The vegan PDRN" / "PDRN without the salmon"
- "Snail mucin alternative for vegans"
- Tallow as bioidentical/ancestral vs seed-oil drugstore moisturizers
- Seed-oil-free, non-toxic, EWG-style avoidance reasoning

## Banned / forbidden
- No medical claims (treat/cure/heal conditions). No "eliminates wrinkles" — use supports/visibly reduces language cautiously.
- Never say the serum "isn't vegan-adjacent salmon" sloppily — the claim is "non-salmon-derived, vegan PDRN"; say "rare," not "the only" (superlative unverified).
- No fake urgency, fake scarcity, or invented reviews/testimonials (FTC). Social proof: 4.5★ Judge.me rating on tallow (small review count — don't inflate).
- Don't call the brand "clean vegan." Don't use "health-conscious moms."
- No sitewide-sale framing, no "prices slashed," no discounts other than CLEAN15 first-order.
