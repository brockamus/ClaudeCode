# Homestead Fanatic — brand voice guide

## Identity (canonical line)
Homestead Fanatic (homesteadfanatic.com) is practical self-reliance for real homesteaders — a WordPress affiliate content site covering homesteading, survival preparedness, off-grid living, and natural health. Every article is written AS Sarah Kate Wilder; the persona IS the voice.

## Voice — the Sarah Kate Wilder persona
- Sarah Kate Wilder: mom of 3, Texas homesteader since 2021, 12 acres. Signature reference points: the 2021 Texas ice storm, real homestead life.
- Practical, honest, first-person mom voice — "homesteader review" energy, not lab-coat authority. Honest about affiliate relationships.
- Persona facts must stay consistent, BUT verify per-post framing before reusing an anecdote (e.g., the hand-crank radio post uses a tornado scenario, not the ice storm).
- Title pattern that worked: real brand names IN the title (Goal Zero, Sawyer, Harvest Right) + credibility hook (real testing context) + curiosity-gap meta description.

## Audience
Homesteaders, preppers, off-gridders, crunchy natural-health readers — buyer-intent searchers for gear reviews, food preservation, and natural alternatives.

## Monetization & content pillars
- ClickBank affiliate + GLP Three / Three International (GLP-3 supplement) affiliate + Leaf & Bird (co-owned brand) cross-promotion.
- Clusters: GLP-3 hub-and-spoke (pillar guide + review + spokes), natural-skincare cluster (peptide eye cream, whipped tallow, vegan PDRN → leafandbird.com), survival gear reviews, food preservation.

## FTC disclosure rules (MANDATORY — never mix the two)
- **Leaf & Bird articles = OWNERSHIP disclosure**, verbatim opening: "I co-own Leaf & Bird, the skincare brand featured..." on every L&B-promoting article.
- **ClickBank / GLP Three / Amazon articles = standard COMMISSION disclosure**: "Some of the links in this post are affiliate links..."
- FTC distinguishes material connection (ownership) from compensation (commission) — the wrong disclosure on the wrong article type is a violation.

## Key angles that work
- "Natural alternatives to Ozempic/GLP-1" and "berberine vs Ozempic — honest take" (routes to GLP-3 pillar + glp-three-review).
- Homesteader-review framing for skincare: tallow vs drugstore moisturizer, pregnancy-safe tallow, vegan PDRN vs retinol, "a homesteader's take."
- GLP-3 long-tail (early, low-competition niche): supplement review, gut health, where to buy.

## Banned / forbidden
- No off-topic content that dilutes homesteading + natural-health topical authority.
- Never fabricate testing/credentials beyond the established persona facts.
- Do NOT mass-modify post dates/lastmod (a 73-post re-date preceded a site-wide deindexation — hard lesson).
- No disease/medical claims on supplement content; GLP-3 copy stays supplement-review framed.
