# Patriot Feed Shop — brand voice guide

## Identity (canonical line)
Patriot Feed Shop is America's boldest patriotic shirts & gifts — a straight conservative/pro-American apparel store (Patriot Depot competitor) at shop.patriotfeed.org. Hero line: "WEAR WHAT YOU BELIEVE." Section motto: "AMERICAN MADE. AMERICAN PROUD."

NOT a news-merch brand — the old "merch at the speed of news" / "headline to hanger" positioning was killed. patriotfeed.org (the news site) is a traffic asset, not the identity.

## Voice
- Bold satire + faith-forward. Confident, declarative, unapologetic — Trump/anti-left satire, Christian faith, 2A, America 250 (semiquincentennial), military/veteran pride.
- True Classic Tees-style presentation: punchy headlines, title-case CTAs, clean product-first copy.
- Emails frame 250th-themed products as "anniversary drops" / "250th Anniversary Collection."
- Meta ads: product-only copy (no divisive-political messaging in paid creative — the "copy firewall").

## Audience
Conservative, Christian, patriotic Americans — Patriot Depot's crowd. Vets, gun owners, faith-and-family buyers. Black is the #1 selling shirt color; core lineup dark shirts with cream/red ink.

## Hero products & prices
- Graphic tees (Bella 3001, US-printed): $24.95 / $26.95 / $28.95 by size tier. ~34 active products.
- Design catalog examples: 250 Years of Freedom, We The People, Faith Over Fear, God Guns & Glory, Come and Take It (2A), I Identify As A Patriot, Powered By Liberal Tears, Jesus & America Forever, Fight Fight Fight, One Nation Under God, Built on Faith Forged in Freedom.
- **Patriot Shirt Club** (subscription, /pages/patriot-shirt-club): exclusive tee monthly featuring "a different pro-American or veteran-owned business, every month." Monthly $29 (2XL $31, 3XL $33, 4XL $35); prepaid ladder shown as ~$28/mo (3-mo), ~$27/mo (6-mo), ~$26/mo (12-mo). Vault = à-la-carte past/house designs.

## Offers (strict rules)
- WELCOME10 — 10% off, once per customer, for footer email signups. Only evergreen code.
- Club has NO intro discount by design (flat price, matches Grunt Style/Gym Shirt Club model); prepaid terms are the only savings.
- Mission hook: "a portion of every order" supports Untrafficked (child anti-trafficking) — say "a portion," never a specific % .

## Key angles that work
- America 250 / 250th anniversary (July 4, 2026) — proven top-seller theme.
- Faith + freedom pairings; 2A heritage; "wear what you believe" identity.
- Club: back a different small pro-American or veteran-owned business each month (tribal/affinity, anti-big-brand).

## Banned / forbidden
- NEVER claim the club or brand is veteran-owned (Brock is not a veteran). Correct phrasing: "backing veteran-owned & pro-American business." Lead with "pro-American" before "veteran-owned."
- No fake ratings/reviews (theme's fake 4.7 stars were deliberately removed — reviews app not yet installed).
- No news-cycle/"speed of news" framing.
- No exact prepaid dollar totals in copy (rounded $28/$27/$26 + frequency only — real totals differ slightly).
