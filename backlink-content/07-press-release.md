## Nature-Published Study Shows Triple-Agonist GLP-3 Approach Reduces Cancer Tumors Up to 17x — Homestead Fanatic Publishes Free Consumer Guide Breaking Down the Research

*As consumer interest in natural GLP-3 alternatives surges, health resource site Homestead Fanatic releases comprehensive guide library helping readers understand the science behind the next generation of weight management*

**WIMBERLEY, TX — March 27, 2026** — A study published in Nature's *npj Metabolic Health and Disease* has revealed that retatrutide, the triple-agonist drug commonly referred to as "GLP-3," reduced pancreatic cancer tumor volume by 14-fold and lung cancer tumor volume by 17-fold in pre-clinical models — dramatically outperforming the single-agonist drug semaglutide (marketed as Ozempic), which achieved only a 4-fold reduction in pancreatic cancer models.

The research, conducted by scientists at Vanderbilt University and the University of Pennsylvania and published on March 14, 2025 (DOI: 10.1038/s44324-025-00054-5), also found that the anti-cancer immune benefits persisted even after treatment was discontinued and weight was regained — suggesting the triple-pathway approach triggers durable immune system reprogramming beyond weight loss alone.

In response to surging consumer interest in GLP-3 and natural alternatives to pharmaceutical weight management, health and homesteading resource site **Homestead Fanatic** (https://homesteadfanatic.com) has published a free, comprehensive guide library covering the science, safety, dosage, and consumer options for GLP-3.

"The conversation around weight management is evolving fast, and most people are getting their information from pharmaceutical marketing or social media clips," said **Sarah Kate Wilder**, founder of Homestead Fanatic. "We saw a need for straightforward, well-researched content that helps people understand what GLP-3 actually is, how it compares to current options like Ozempic, and what natural alternatives are available right now — without the hype."

**Key resources published by Homestead Fanatic include:**

- **GLP-3 and Retatrutide Explained** — a comprehensive breakdown of the triple-agonist mechanism, clinical trial results, and what consumers need to know (https://homesteadfanatic.com/glp3-retatrutide-explained/)

- **GLP-3 Retatrutide Cancer Study Analysis** — a consumer-accessible summary of the Nature-published research and its implications (https://homesteadfanatic.com/glp3-retatrutide-cancer-study/)

- **GLP-3 Dosage Guide** — clinical dosing data from published trials alongside natural supplement usage guidelines (https://homesteadfanatic.com/glp3-dosage-guide/)

The guide library addresses a growing information gap as searches for "GLP-3," "natural Ozempic alternative," and "retatrutide" have increased significantly throughout 2025 and 2026. With pharmaceutical retatrutide still in Phase 3 clinical trials and not expected to reach the market before late 2026 or 2027, consumers are actively seeking reliable information about both the pharmaceutical pipeline and natural supplement alternatives currently available.

"Over 40 percent of American adults are classified as obese, and obesity is linked to at least thirteen types of cancer," Wilder added. "When a Nature-published study shows a triple-pathway approach reducing tumors by 14 to 17 times more than the leading drug on the market, that deserves serious attention — and people deserve access to clear, honest information about it."
